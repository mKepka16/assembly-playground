5.1
Register is volatile when according to programming calling convention under use - it can be freely modified by any subroutine. Calling subroutine may alter its value.
In ARM calling convention registers r0-r3 (a1-a4) are considered volatile. Mistake: I forgot about r12 - intra procedure scratch register.

5.2
Static variables live for the entire program duration. This includes variables in .data and .bss section.
Automatic variables are created in runtime - they can be either on stack, or directly in the register.
Usually, lifespan of automatic variables is constained by subroutine execution context.
